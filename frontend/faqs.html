How our acquisition teams support investors, wholesalers and agents.

VoloLeads builds managed seller acquisition teams for real estate wholesalers, investors and agents in the United States. We provide trained real estate cold callers and acquisition VAs, skip-traced seller lead generation, CRM setup in GoHighLevel or Podio, comps and deal analysis, and AI workflow automation. Every caller is overseen by a VA manager with daily QA and KPI reporting.

Most agencies train agents to read. Our VAs not only utilise evidence-based scripts but are also trained to connect with clients. We prioritise conversation quality over Dial Count. Our internal QA team monitors calls to ensure agents are having real human interactions, not just hitting quotas.

When your scope includes Virtual Drivers for Dollars, our team reviews available property imagery for visible signs of distress. It adds context to outreach; it does not replace a property inspection.

We assess callers on seller conversations, qualification and clear handoffs. They train on motivation, timeline, condition and price, with daily QA to support your campaign’s criteria.

We track everything using our Daily Performance KPIs:

  • Pipeline Velocity: How fast are leads moving from "new" to "contract"?
  • Contact & Pick-Up Rates: Are we calling at the right times?
  • Talk Time vs. Dial Time: Are VAs having conversations or just hearing dial tones?
  • Call-to-Appointment Ratio: How many conversations turn into scheduled appointments?
  • Lead Quality: We QC (Quality Control) every appointment to ensure it meets your campaign criteria.

These are sourced records, not qualified opportunities or guaranteed results. The bonus is not a monthly allowance and does not include ongoing free lead replenishment. We use our concession method. Our agents are trained never to argue. When a seller yells, our agents agree ("You're right, spammers are annoying") to lower the seller's guard, then pivot back to the evidence ("I only called because I saw the roof damage"). We turn "No" into a conversation, or we disqualify them immediately so you don't waste time on retail buyers.

Most campaigns generate 30–50+ seller leads per month. The exact number depends on the quality of your lists and whether SMS workflows are part of your campaign. Results vary by market and list type.

Our calling procedures are built around the TCPA, Do Not Call rules and state calling laws:

  • Lists are screened against national and applicable state Do Not Call lists, with DNC checks refreshed daily before each calling session.
  • Calling hours are set per market from written rules.
  • Outbound calls are made by trained human callers, never AI-generated or prerecorded voices.
  • Owners who ask us to stop are marked Do Not Contact and excluded from future lists.
  • SMS runs only on contacts with documented opt-in consent, and STOP replies end messaging immediately.

Read the full detail in our Compliance Policy and our TCPA compliance guide. This is a summary of our procedures, not legal advice.

Follow-up and closing are managed by our clients, so closing outcomes are not consistently linked to the leads we deliver. Our job is to find motivated sellers, qualify them on Motivation, Timeline, Condition and Price, and hand them to your team with full notes. How quickly and how well each lead is followed up has a big effect on whether it closes.

We pull government records directly from the county, such as tax delinquencies, probates and code violations, and then skip trace them through a data provider to find contact details for the most motivated sellers. The records are cleaned before they reach the dialer and are screened against Do Not Call lists before calling.

Everything is set up within 2–3 days. After checkout you complete the intake form and share access to your CRM and dialer. We then configure your script, lead criteria and routing, and calling begins as soon as setup is complete.

A real estate cold calling VA (virtual assistant) calls property owners from your seller lists, finds the ones who are motivated to sell, and qualifies them on Motivation, Timeline, Condition and Price (MTCP). Qualified seller leads are sent to your CRM with full notes, so your acquisitions team only follows up with sellers worth talking to. VoloLeads callers work Eastern time hours on a WAVV triple-line dialer, with daily call reviews and coaching from a VA manager.

We automate the work around the call: moving data between your CRM and dialer, KPI tracking and reports, list pulling and cleaning, callback routing and call QA summaries. On the Scale plan, AI also handles SMS replies and answers inbound seller calls as a voice receptionist. Outbound seller calls always stay human. See AI Workflows for details.
