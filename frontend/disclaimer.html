Results Disclaimer

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

1. No Guarantees of Financial Success or Closed Deals

VoloLeads provides a turnkey growth infrastructure, including pre-configured CRM environments, predictive dialers, clean data pipelines, and fully trained, managed Virtual Assistant (VA) cold callers.

VoloLeads does not, under any circumstances, guarantee deals, revenue, closed contracts, or specific financial outcomes. Our service mandate is strictly limited to identifying, qualifying, and delivering high-intent, motivated leads into your sales pipeline based on industry-standard criteria. The generation of a qualified lead does not equal a closed transaction.

2. Client Execution & Closing Responsibilities

Real estate wholesaling and investing are highly execution-dependent operations. Transforming a qualified lead into a profitable, revenue-generating asset requires continuous human intervention and sales proficiency. Securing a deal under contract and successfully closing it depends entirely on the client's ability to execute the following sales actions:

  • Persistent Follow-Ups: Engaging the prospect multiple times across various communication channels to maintain momentum.
  • Rapport Building: Establishing strategic trust and professional alignment with the property seller.
  • Advanced Negotiation: Navigating motivations, handling objections, and structuring mutually beneficial offers.
  • Deal Underwriting: Accurately calculating After-Repair Value (ARV), structural repair estimates, and maximum allowable offers (MAO).
  • Contracting & Disposition: Getting the property legally locked under contract and successfully marketing it to end-buyers.

3. The Pipeline Velocity Mandate (Lead Decay Clause)

Speed to lead is the single most critical variable in outbound lead generation. VoloLeads VAs inject highly motivated prospects into your CRM in real-time.

Immediate Action Required: You are required to follow up on all submitted leads immediately upon transmission.

Lead Stagnation: Allowing a live lead to sit unattended in your pipeline drastically diminishes its conversion probability. Stagnant leads rapidly cool down, suffer from decreased responsiveness, and are highly likely to engage with competing investors.

VoloLeads absorbs your management, data waterfall, and dialing infrastructure overhead — but you are the closer. Your pipeline velocity determines your acquisition ROI.
