Compliance

Our approach to responsible calling, SMS outreach, and client accountability.

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

VoloLeads is a registered partnership based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, operated by Syed and Ramisa. We provide virtual assistant, lead qualification, and campaign support services for real estate investors working in selected US markets.

Our approach combines daily Do Not Call screening, trained human callers, respectful conversations, opt-out handling, and campaign reporting. This page explains those practices, the client’s responsibilities, and the contractual protection VoloLeads does and does not offer.

1. Campaign planning and market selection

Before starting a campaign, we discuss the states and markets the client wants to target, the campaign objectives, and the services VoloLeads will provide.

Campaign preparation includes reviewing applicable state requirements, setting calling hours, and establishing scripts and operating instructions for the selected markets. Our team follows written calling-hour rules and campaign procedures.

2. Contact sourcing and daily DNC screening

We obtain property-owner contact information through third-party property-data and skip-tracing providers, including XLeads, DealMachine, and PropStream.

Skip tracing helps identify contact information. It does not itself establish permission to call or text someone.

We screen contacts against national and applicable state Do Not Call lists before exporting a campaign's calling list, also called a smart list. We refresh those checks daily before each calling session.

We also exclude contacts previously marked Do Not Contact or Not Interested in our campaign records.

3. Human-led calling and respectful conversations

Our outbound calling campaigns use trained human virtual assistants working through third-party dialing platforms. These calling conversations do not use AI-generated or prerecorded voices.

Our VAs explain that they are virtual assistants calling on behalf of a partner interested in purchasing property in the area. They do not represent themselves as the buyer.

The purpose of the conversation is to find out whether the owner is considering selling. Our VAs do not pressure owners, argue with them, or continue trying to persuade someone who has declined.

When an owner expresses interest, the VA provides the name of the partner who will follow up and gathers information to help qualify the opportunity.

Campaign scripts and caller identification must meet the requirements applicable to the campaign.

4. How we qualify interested property owners

For owners willing to discuss a potential sale, our VAs gather information about:

  • Motivation: Why the owner is considering selling.
  • Condition: The property's condition and any known repair needs.
  • Timeline: When the owner would like to sell.
  • Price: The owner's asking price or price expectations.

These details help the client evaluate the opportunity and prepare for follow-up. Qualification does not guarantee the accuracy of every statement an owner provides, a signed purchase agreement, or a completed transaction.

5. Respecting requests to stop contact

If an owner says they are not interested, the VA respectfully ends the conversation and marks the contact Not Interested to prevent further calling.

If someone asks to be removed from the list or requests no further contact, the VA marks the contact Do Not Contact. Our team also ends the conversation and applies this designation when an owner becomes aggravated.

These statuses are used to exclude contacts from future calling lists managed for the campaign.

6. SMS campaigns and client-supplied consent

Clients are responsible for obtaining their SMS sending numbers, completing applicable brand and campaign registration—including A2P 10DLC registration where required—and supplying contacts with appropriate, documented opt-in consent.

A purchased or skip-traced phone number, absence from a DNC list, or an approved sending number does not replace recipient consent.

Before a drip campaign starts, VoloLeads reviews the supplied list and consent information and sets up the CRM workflow, message sequence, and AI-assisted reply handling.

SMS messages must identify the sender and provide clear unsubscribe instructions, such as “Reply STOP to unsubscribe.”

Our SMS workflows are configured to process STOP replies and other requests to end contact immediately, stop the messaging sequence, flag the contact as Do Not Contact, and remove the contact from active outreach lists.

Clients must supply accurate consent information and honor suppression statuses in any follow-up they conduct independently.

7. Training, monitoring, and reporting

Our team uses written scripts, calling-hour rules, VA training, and third-party monitoring software to oversee campaign activity.

Clients receive monthly reports containing:

  • VA timesheets.
  • Campaign key performance indicators.
  • Outbound and inbound call history.
  • Smart lists prepared for the campaign.

As of October 28, 2026, VoloLeads reports no complaints alleging failure to follow its agreed calling procedures. This statement describes our reported history and is not a guarantee of future results or the absence of future complaints.

8. Client responsibilities and property transactions

Clients are responsible for evaluating submitted leads, conducting due diligence, approving offers, entering into property agreements, and completing transactions.

Agreements between property owners and the client—including disputes about offers, disclosures, payment, performance, or closing—remain the responsibility of the parties to those agreements.

VoloLeads provides lead qualification and campaign support. Providing these services does not make VoloLeads the property buyer or a party to a property transaction.

9. Acquisition support under the Scale plan

An acquisition manager provided through the Scale plan supports the client's business within the agreed scope and authority.

The client remains responsible for its purchasing decisions, approved offers, transaction documents, and obligations to property owners. Providing an acquisition manager does not make VoloLeads a party to the client's purchase agreements.

10. Third-party platforms

We use third-party data providers, dialers, CRMs, and communications services to support campaign delivery.

Features provided by these platforms assist our operating procedures. Using a platform or purchasing a sending number does not, by itself, establish consent, guarantee compliance, or provide indemnification.

Any protection offered by a third-party provider depends on that provider's applicable written agreement.

VoloLeads commits to following its agreed campaign procedures. It does not provide contractual indemnification or promise to reimburse clients for claims, fines, settlements, damages, or legal costs associated with calling or SMS campaigns.

Our procedures are intended to reduce risk and support responsible outreach. They do not guarantee that a campaign will never receive a complaint or legal claim.

This provision describes the contractual protection VoloLeads offers. It does not determine responsibility under applicable law, prevent a claim from being brought, or exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
