Before a campaign begins, we discuss the client’s target states and markets and review the requirements applicable to the campaign. Our team follows written calling-hour rules, scripts, training, and agreed campaign instructions.

We use third-party property-data and skip-tracing providers to obtain contact information. We screen contacts against national and applicable state Do Not Call lists before exporting a calling list and refresh those checks daily before each calling session.

Our outbound calling conversations are conducted by trained human virtual assistants through third-party dialers. These conversations do not use AI-generated or prerecorded voices. VAs explain that they are virtual assistants calling on behalf of a potential property buyer and do not claim to be the buyer. Campaign scripts and caller identification must meet applicable requirements.

If an owner is not interested, the VA respectfully ends the call and marks the contact Not Interested to prevent further calling. Requests to stop contact, and calls where an owner becomes aggravated, are marked Do Not Contact. These statuses exclude contacts from future calling lists managed for the campaign.
