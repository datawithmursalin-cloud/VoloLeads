Essential includes a one-time signup bonus of 10,000 leads sourced using BatchLeads at no additional charge. It is not a monthly allowance. Growth and Scale include the monthly lead-sourcing services listed in their plans.

You can manage cancellation and renewal through the billing portal. All plans are billed monthly. Essential is $6 / hour, billed $730 monthly, plus a $150 one-time setup fee. Billing terms are shown at checkout.
